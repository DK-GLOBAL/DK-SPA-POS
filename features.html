Your data stays protected

Each spa has its own separate data space. DK Global Tech cannot edit your sales, clients, staff or prices. Everyone signs in with their own password, accounts lock after repeated wrong passwords, and sensitive actions are written to an audit log. We handle data in line with the principles of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

A once-off setup fee and a monthly subscription, agreed with DK Global Tech and written in your agreement. WhatsApp us for the current rates.

Can I take M-Pesa payments?

Yes. Cash, M-Pesa and card are recorded at the counter, and DK Global Tech sets up your M-Pesa Daraja details. Always confirm the M-Pesa message before you record a payment.

Where is my data kept and who can see it?

Each spa has its own separate data space, kept in the browser of the device you sign in on, so download a data file often as a backup. Only your own team can see it, according to the rights your manager gives. DK Global Tech cannot edit it.

What if I forget my password?

Staff request a one-time code (OTP) that the manager sees and passes on. Managers contact DK Global Tech by WhatsApp or email. Passwords are never sent by email.

Do you issue receipts and invoices?

Yes. Receipts and invoices can be printed or saved as PDF in 58mm, 80mm, A5 or A4, with your business details, and your KRA PIN and VAT if you want them.

Does it work in Kiswahili?

Yes. Use the language box at the top of the page to switch between English and Kiswahili.
